Four studios, one paper surface

They share the same paper theme, the same undo and the same private storage. Start in one, finish in another.

  1. Photo studio الصور

    Light, colour and detail, without a subscription window in the way.

    • Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, temperature, saturation and detail
    • Original film-style looks; crop, rotate and straighten
    • Arabic and English text, stickers and freehand drawing, with undo and redo
    • JPEG or PNG export at Original, 2048 or 1080 px — “Largest” is capped at 3200 px
    • Collage mode: layouts and gutters, exported as one image

  2. Design studio التصميم

    A blank canvas and seven original layered templates.

    • Post, story, landscape and A4 canvases from a single blank canvas
    • Seven original templates: event poster, recipe card, product label, countdown card and more
    • Image, text and shape layers; move, scale and rotate them
    • PNG or JPEG export from the same studio as the rest

  3. Video studio الفيديو

    Trim, reorder and title up to five clips — exported on the device.

    • Up to 5 clips and 3 minutes per video project
    • Trim, reorder, colour look, frame ratio and a title
    • Exact full-project preview at a 960 px proxy; export quality is unchanged
    • On-device MP4 export with Media3 — no upload queue to sit in
    • No music library or audio import in this version; a clip's own audio is kept

  4. AI Lab مختبر الذكاء

    Remove, blur or replace a background without sending the photo anywhere.

    • Six-model catalogue: two bundled offline, four optional downloads
    • MediaPipe Selfie Segmenter (square and landscape), Hair Segmenter and Multiclass
    • DeepLab-V3 for general objects, pets, products and scenery — not portraits
    • MODNet for portraits only, with soft hair edges
    • Downloads use HTTPS and are verified by SHA-256 before use

Projects and results that stay put

Save, reopen, rename or delete a photo edit, design, cut-out or video project. Projects live in the app's private storage — never exported, synced or transferred. After every export you get a result card with a preview, the filename, where it was saved, and Open, Share and Close.

The honest limits: exports are flattened, so camera EXIF and the original colour profile are not kept; the “Largest” export is capped at 3200 px; video projects stop at 3 minutes or 5 clips; and there is no cloud sync, project file export or project transfer between devices.

One permission. No account. No telemetry.

Midad Studio declares exactly one Android permission and uses it for one thing: downloading an optional AI model when you ask. There is no account to create, no ad to watch and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK inside the app.

android.permission.INTERNET optional model downloads only

What never happens

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads and no advertising ID
  • No analytics, telemetry or session recording
  • No crash-reporting SDK
  • No watermark on anything you make
  • No cloud sync
  • No upload of your media
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts or broad photo-library access

What stays on your phone

  • Photos and videos you edit, read through Android's photo picker
  • Project records and thumbnails in app-private storage
  • Exports in your own gallery, as normal files
  • Downloaded models, checked by SHA-256 before use
  • Android backup and device transfer, both disabled

Questions, answered

For anything else, the issue tracker is the support channel.

Is Midad Studio free?

Yes. Downloading and using Midad Studio 0.2.3 is free. There is no account, no ad and no watermark.

Where do I download it?

From GitHub Releases: the signed APK for v0.2.3. Midad Studio is not on Google Play yet — this is a pre-release sideload build, and nothing has been submitted to Play Console.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, not for editing. Every studio works offline. The only network request is an optional AI-model download that you start yourself; it uses HTTPS and the file is checked against a published SHA-256 before it is used.

What permissions does it request?

One: android.permission.INTERNET, used only for optional AI-model downloads. The app does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or full photo-library access; media is chosen through Android's photo picker.

What happens to my photos and projects?

They stay on your phone. Editing runs on the device, projects are stored in app-private storage, and Android backup and device transfer are disabled. There is no cloud sync and nothing is uploaded to the developer.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. The 0.2.3 build targets Android 16 (API 36), is minified with R8 and ships baseline profiles.

What are the export and video limits?

Video projects are capped at 5 clips and 3 minutes, previewed at a 960 px proxy and exported as MP4 on the device. Photo and design exports are JPEG or PNG with the longest side at Original, 2048 or 1080 px — the Largest option is capped at 3200 px. Exports are flattened, so camera EXIF and the original colour profile are not preserved. There is no music library or audio import in this version.

Is the source code open source?

The source is public to read, but the licence is all rights reserved — source available, not an open-source licence. Third-party libraries and AI models keep their own licences, most of them Apache-2.0.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Arabic is the app's primary language, with a full right-to-left interface; English is included too, and this website mirrors that in both directions.
