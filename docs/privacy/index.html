Privacy Policy — English

Policy version 4.0. This policy applies to the Android application Midad Studio (package com.midad.studio), version 0.2.3 (versionCode 5), and to any later version that links to this page.

Where this policy lives. This page is the same text published at https://ahmadmhmdsy.github.io/midad-studio/privacy/ and kept in the source repository at docs/privacy/index.html; the hosted copy is the version of record. The in-app privacy summary (Settings → Privacy) is a short notice, not the policy. Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent through the project's GitHub Issues.

At a glance: editing happens on your phone, there is no account, no ads and no analytics, and the only network request is an optional AI-model download you start yourself. The full text follows.

Who we are

  • Publisher / data controller: Ahmad Mahmoud
  • Privacy contact: the project's GitHub Issues
  • App: Midad Studio for Android, package com.midad.studio.

"We" and "us" below mean that publisher. We operate no server, no account system and no way to identify you from the app. There is no developer-side database about users of this app, because no user data is ever sent to us.

The short version

  • Your photos and videos are edited on your phone and stay there. Nothing you edit is uploaded to us.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app declares one permission: internet access, used only when you ask it to download an optional AI model file.
  • Android backup is switched off for the app, so the app's data is not copied to Google Drive and does not move to a new phone through the operating system.
  • You can remove everything by deleting it inside the app or by uninstalling the app. Exports you saved to your gallery are your files and stay until you delete them.

2What we never do

  • No account, sign-in, profile, user identifier or device fingerprint.
  • No advertising, ad SDK, advertising ID or attribution SDK.
  • No analytics, telemetry, event logging, session recording, heat maps or A/B testing.
  • No crash-reporting SDK: if the app crashes, the operating system may write a local log, but nothing is sent to us and we have no way to read it.
  • No upload of your media, projects, exports, thumbnails or model choices to us or to any third party.
  • No sale, rent or "sharing" of personal data as Google Play defines it.
  • No profiling, no automated decision-making, and no training of AI models on your content.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, SMS, call log or phone state.

3Network use and recipients

The app makes one kind of network request, and only when you start it:

  1. An optional AI model download. The AI Lab lists models from a catalogue shipped inside the app (assets/models/manifest.json). Models marked as bundled are already in the app and never need the network. A model marked for download is fetched only after you tap download. The request is a plain HTTPS GET for one file, issued by the app's own downloader (core/ai/internal/DefaultModelRepository.kt). It sends:
    • the URL of the model file,
    • a fixed User-Agent string identifying the app,
    • an Accept: application/octet-stream header,
    • no cookies of our own, and no identifier, account token, advertising ID or device ID.
    The HTTP stack may add the standard headers any HTTPS client sends. Your IP address is visible to the host, as it is for any download. The file is streamed to a temporary .part file, checked against the SHA-256 checksum recorded in the app's catalogue, and only installed if the checksum matches.

Current hosts: the download URLs in the shipped catalogue point to Google Cloud Storage (storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/…) for the MediaPipe models and to Hugging Face (huggingface.co/litert-community/…) for the MODNet portrait-matting model. Each host processes standard connection metadata under its own privacy policy. We receive nothing back from either host: no download receipt, no file list, no identifier.

There are no other endpoints. No cloud sync, no remote configuration, no push messaging, no licence check, no update check, no A/B service, no error-reporting endpoint, and no background traffic of any kind.

4Permissions

The 0.2.3 manifest declares exactly one user-facing permission.

PermissionWhy it is declaredWhen it is used
android.permission.INTERNETTo download an optional AI model file that you explicitly requestOnly during that download; never for media, telemetry, ads or updates

How the app works without storage, camera or microphone permissions:

  • Media is chosen with the Android system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia / PickMultipleVisualMedia). The picker returns access to only the items you select; the app cannot browse or index your library.
  • Exports go to your gallery through MediaStore, which needs no storage permission on Android 10+. On Android 7.0–9.0 (API 26–28) the same MediaStore path is used where available, and sharing a file from app-specific storage goes through the app's own non-exported FileProvider.
  • No camera and no microphone are requested: the app imports media through the picker and never records.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, SMS or phone-state access is requested.

Technical footnote for reviewers: the merged manifest also contains com.midad.studio.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, a signature-level permission that AndroidX declares for the app itself so that its dynamically registered broadcast receivers are not exported to other apps. It is not a runtime permission, it is not grantable to other apps, and it gives access to no user data. It is not a data-collection permission and it is not requested from the user.

Earlier drafts of this policy described media or storage permissions and an implied backup story. Those statements did not match the shipped manifest; this version replaces them. The source manifest in app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml is the authority: it declares INTERNET and explicitly removes the media, storage, foreground-service and wake-lock permissions that libraries would otherwise merge in.

5Local storage, retention and deletion

Retention. Everything the app stores is on your device and stays until you remove it:

  • Project records, thumbnails and settings: until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app.
  • AI model files: until you delete the model in the AI Lab's model manager, or uninstall the app. Deleting a model is immediate and cannot be undone; the model can be installed again later.
  • Exports: they are ordinary files in your gallery and remain until you delete them with the gallery, a file manager or the app's Projects screen (which deletes the export it created).
  • Server-side: none. There is no retention period because there is no server-side store. We hold no copy of your media, your projects, your settings or your exports.

Deletion.

  • In-app: delete a project or an export from Projects; delete an installed AI model in the AI Lab's model manager.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes its private storage, including project records, settings, thumbnails and installed models. Files you exported to the gallery are not removed by uninstalling — they are yours and must be deleted from the gallery.
  • Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. If you open a request in GitHub Issues asking us to delete data, we will explain that we hold none and help you delete the local copy.

6Backup, device transfer and copies

  • Android backup is disabled for the app: android:allowBackup="false" in the manifest, and the app's data-extraction rules exclude every domain from both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Thumbnails are written to a directory that is excluded from backup.
  • Downloaded or copied AI models contain no user data; they are re-installable at any time and are not part of any backup because backup is off.
  • Your exports are ordinary gallery files. Whether your own device backup copies them is decided by your Google or device backup settings, not by us.

7Children

Midad Studio is a general-purpose photo, video, collage and design editor. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not marketed to them. There are no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no social features, no messaging and no data collection that could identify a child. A child who uses the app edits on the device only; nothing is sent to us. If you believe a child's personal data has reached us, open a request in GitHub Issues and we will act on it; in practice we hold no such data.

8Why we are allowed to process it (lawful basis)

Where the EU/UK GDPR or a similar law applies:

  • Performance of the service you asked for — Article 6(1)(b): reading the item you pick, letting you edit it and writing the export you requested. This happens entirely on your device.
  • Consent — Article 6(1)(a): the optional model download starts only when you tap download. You can cancel it, delete the model afterwards, or use the app with the network off. Withdrawing consent is simply not downloading.
  • Legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f): keeping the app secure and working, for example verifying a model's checksum before use. Because nothing is transmitted to us, this interest is narrow.
  • Legal obligation — Article 6(1)(c): only if we were compelled to answer a lawful request. We hold no server-side data to produce.

Special-category data. We do not ask for it. A photo may contain your face, your family, your home or health information; that content is processed only on your device, at your direction, and is never transmitted to us.

No automated decisions. We do not profile you and no decision about you is automated.

9Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access what is held about you — in practice everything in §1, and it is all on your device.
  • Erasure — delete a project, delete a model, or uninstall the app.
  • Portability — your exports are standard JPEG, PNG or MP4 files that you can copy anywhere.
  • Restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent — stop using the optional download, cancel a download, delete the model, or use the app with the network off.
  • Complain to your national data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, open a request in GitHub Issues. We answer within 30 days. Because we operate no server and hold no identifier, we may be unable to locate any data about you; in that case we will say so plainly rather than pretend otherwise.

10Security

  • No server, so no server breach. The strongest protection is that the data never leaves your phone.
  • Media access is limited to the items you pick with the system Photo Picker; there is no broad library access.
  • App data sits in Android's app-private storage, protected by the operating system's app sandbox.
  • Model downloads use HTTPS only (redirects to plain HTTP are refused), and each file is verified against a SHA-256 checksum before it is used; a mismatch discards the file.
  • Backup and device-transfer extraction are disabled, so the system does not copy app data off the device.
  • Release builds are minified with R8 and resource shrinking, only the launcher activity is exported, and the FileProvider used for legacy sharing is not exported.
  • Limits we cannot remove: a rooted or compromised device, another app with unusually broad access, or files you deliberately share through another app.

11International transfers

We do not transfer personal data internationally, because we do not receive any. If you start a model download, your request goes to the host named in §3, which may be outside your country; that request is made by you to the host and is governed by the host's own terms and safeguards.

12Third-party components and models

The app includes open-source libraries — LiteRT, Media3, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and AndroidX — and openly licensed segmentation models, all under licences that require no data collection. Their licences and attributions are listed in Settings → Licenses and in MODEL_LICENSES.md. They are software components, not data-collecting services: none of them contains analytics, advertising or tracking code.

A segmentation model that is not bundled with the app is installed only if you install it, and its licence is supplied by whoever publishes that model — not by us.

13Store and operating-system data

Google Play and Android may collect install, crash, performance and diagnostic information under their own policies if you install the app through a store. The app ships no analytics or crash SDK, so none of that reporting comes from instrumentation inside the app, and we do not receive analytics or crash data from it.

3الشبكة والمستلمون

يُجري التطبيق نوعاً واحداً من طلبات الشبكة، ويبدأه أنت فقط:

  • تنزيل اختياري لنموذج ذكاء اصطناعي. تُقرأ قائمة النماذج من كتالوج مضمَّن في التطبيق (assets/models/manifest.json). النماذج المضمَّنة موجودة داخل التطبيق ولا تحتاج الشبكة. والنموذج المُعلَّم للتنزيل لا يُجلب إلا بعد ضغطك على زر التنزيل: طلب GET واحد عبر HTTPS لملف واحد، يرسل عنوان الملف ووكيل مستخدم ثابتاً وترويسة Accept، دون أي كوكيز خاصة بنا ودون أي معرّف أو رمز حساب. ويظهر عنوان IP الخاص بك للمضيف كما في أي تنزيل. يُحفظ الملف مؤقتاً بامتداد .part، ويُتحقق من بصمة SHA-256 قبل تثبيته.
  • المضيفون الحاليون: عناوين التنزيل في الكتالوج المُضمَّن تشير إلى Google Cloud Storage (storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/…) لنماذج MediaPipe، وإلى Hugging Face (huggingface.co/litert-community/…) لنموذج MODNet لعزل صورة الشخص. ويعالج كل مضيف بيانات الاتصال المعتادة وفق سياسة الخصوصية الخاصة به. ولا نستلم من أي منهما شيئاً.
  • لا نقاط اتصال أخرى: لا مزامنة سحابية، ولا إعدادات عن بُعد، ولا إشعارات، ولا تحقق من ترخيص، ولا فحص تحديثات، ولا أي حركة في الخلفية.

8الأساس القانوني للمعالجة

9حقوقك

بحسب القانون المعمول به، لك الحق في:

  • الوصول إلى ما يُحتفظ عنك، وهو كل ما في البند 1، وموجود على جهازك.
  • المحو بحذف مشروع أو نموذج أو بإزالة التطبيق.
  • النقل لأن تصديراتك ملفات JPEG أو PNG أو MP4 قياسية.
  • تقييد المعالجة أو الاعتراض أو سحب الموافقة بالتوقف عن التنزيل الاختياري أو حذف النموذج أو استخدام التطبيق دون اتصال.

10الأمان

  • لا خادم، فلا اختراق لخادم. الحماية الأقوى هي أن البيانات لا تغادر هاتفك.
  • الوصول إلى الوسائط مقصور على ما تختاره في منتقي الصور.
  • بيانات التطبيق في تخزينه الخاص المحمي بصندوق أندرويد.
  • التنزيل عبر HTTPS فقط (وتُرفض إعادة التوجيه إلى HTTP)، مع التحقق من SHA-256 قبل الاستخدام.
  • النسخ الاحتياطي والنقل بين الأجهزة معطّلان.
